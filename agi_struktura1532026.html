2. Core Platform Modules

AI Chat Engine

Handles website chat, Telegram bot, and future channels like WhatsApp and Messenger. Responsible for conversation flow and AI interaction.

Voice AI Engine

Voice interface allowing customers to speak with an AI assistant that recommends telecom packages and captures leads.

Sales Funnel AI

Guides the customer through structured questions to identify the best telecom package.

User enters site
      ↓
AI asks: Home or Business?
      ↓
How many devices?
      ↓
Need TV channels?
      ↓
Recommended package
      ↓
Lead capture

Knowledge Base

Structured database containing telecom package data, pricing, speeds, coverage, and frequently asked questions.

Lead Management System

Stores all customer interactions across channels and converts them into structured sales leads.

5. AI Callback System (Automated Sales Calls)

This system automatically calls users who leave their phone number and continues the sales conversation using Voice AI.

        ▼

        ▼

        │
        ▼

        │

Example Call Flow

AI: Hello, this is Ana AI from TVInternetSBB.

You recently asked about internet packages.
Would you like help choosing the best option?

Customer: Yes.

AI: Is the internet for home or business?

Customer: Business.

AI: I recommend the Biznis Net 3 package.
Would you like us to schedule installation?

Voice AI Components
